About Greenpath

Green Path is your ultimate guide to exploring the natural beauty of the Auckland region, designed for walkers, hikers, and outdoor enthusiasts of all levels. Whether you’re looking for a peaceful forest stroll, a scenic coastal track, or a more challenging bush adventure, Green Path brings together everything you need in one place.

Our platform features an interactive map that makes it easy to discover bushwalks near you or plan your next outing. Each track is carefully curated with detailed information, including distance, estimated walking time, difficulty level, terrain type, and key highlights along the way. From hidden waterfalls and native bush to panoramic viewpoints, Green Path helps you find experiences that suit your interests and ability.

What sets Green Path apart is its community-driven review system. Walkers can share their firsthand experiences, tips, and photos, giving you real insight into track conditions, accessibility, and what to expect before you go. This helps ensure you’re always prepared and can choose walks that match your preferences.

Green Path is more than just a directory—it’s a tool for connection with nature. By encouraging exploration of local bushwalks, we aim to promote outdoor wellbeing, environmental awareness, and appreciation for Auckland’s unique landscapes.

Whether you’re a beginner taking your first steps into the bush or a seasoned hiker searching for your next adventure, Green Path is here to guide your journey.
